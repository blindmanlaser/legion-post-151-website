Our Legion Family

Post 151 is home to three organizations that work side by side for veterans, their families and Conneaut. Members can use the Post, take part in our events and programs, and help decide how the Post serves the community.

How to Join

  1. Stop by the Post at 272 Broad St, or send us a message, and tell us which group you'd like to join.
  2. Bring proof of eligibility. Veterans bring a DD-214 or other discharge papers. Auxiliary and Sons applicants bring the name of the veteran they're joining through.
  3. Pay your yearly dues and you're in. Current members renew the same way.
