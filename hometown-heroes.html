Tribute Flags

Hometown Heroes

Honor a Conneaut veteran with a banner displayed in our city from Memorial Day through Veterans Day. Flags ordered after July 1st of the year will be displayed the following Memorial Day.

Honor Your Hero

To honor those who have served this great country, American Legion Post 151 is proud to offer Hometown Hero tribute flags to be displayed in our city from Memorial Day to Veterans Day. If you have a loved one who lived in Conneaut, you can have them immortalized on one of these beautiful flags.

$200 per flag, including the mount

Each flag measures 24 × 48 inches and is made of a canvas-like material treated with UV protection. Flags are printed in the USA.

Now accepting orders for all Conneaut veterans: active duty, retired, honorably discharged and deceased.

Each family must provide

  • The veteran's full name
  • Branch of service
  • Years of service (for example, 1965 – 1968)
  • One photo of the veteran in uniform
  • Optional: operation or era (WWI, WWII, Korea, Vietnam, peacetime, etc.)

How to Order

There are two ways to order. Either way, the cost is $200 per flag, paid in cash or by check made out to Sons of the American Legion Post 151.
