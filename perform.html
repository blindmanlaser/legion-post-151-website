Perform at the Post

Bring Your Act to Post 151

We host live music, DJs, karaoke, trivia and special events for our members and the Conneaut community. If you'd like to play at the Post, tell us about your act below and include a link where we can see or hear you.

Our entertainment committee reviews every submission and will reach out about dates that fit.

What helps us decide

  1. A link to video, music or your Facebook page.
  2. The kind of crowd you play for and your style.
  3. Your usual rate and what you bring (sound, lights).
