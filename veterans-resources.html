Help for Veterans & Families

Veterans Resources

In crisis? Help is available right now.

The Veterans Crisis Line is free, confidential and open 24/7 for veterans, service members and the people who care about them. You don't have to be enrolled in VA care.

Call 988, then press 1 Text 838255 Chat online

Close to Home

Ashtabula County Veterans Service Office

Free help from county service officers. Start here for most questions.

  • 1709 E. Prospect Rd., Ashtabula, OH 44004
  • 440-964-8324 · Mon–Fri, 8:00 am – 4:30 pm
  • Help filing VA disability, pension and education claims
  • Emergency help with rent, mortgage, utilities and food for eligible veterans
  • Rides to the Erie and Cleveland VA medical centers
  • Burial benefits and grave markers
  • ashtabulaveterans.com

Ashtabula County VA Clinic

Primary care and other outpatient services, part of VA Erie Health Care.

  • 2044 Lambros Lane, Ashtabula, OH 44004
  • Mon–Fri, 7:30 am – 4:30 pm
  • VA Erie Health Care: 814-868-8661 or 800-274-8387
  • Clinic details on VA.gov

Erie VA Medical Center

The closest VA medical center, about 30 miles east in Erie, Pa.

  • 135 East 38th Street, Erie, PA 16504
  • 814-868-8661 or 800-274-8387
  • VA Erie Health Care on VA.gov

Erie Vet Center

Free, confidential counseling for combat veterans, service members and their families, separate from VA medical records.

  • 240 West 11th Street, Suite 105, Erie, PA 16501
  • Call 877-927-8387 (24/7)
  • Erie Vet Center on VA.gov

Benefits & Help Lines

VA Benefits

Disability compensation, pension, GI Bill, home loans and more.

  • 800-827-1000
  • VA.gov

VA Health Care

Apply for VA health care or ask about eligibility.

  • 877-222-8387
  • VA.gov/health-care

Ohio Department of Veterans Services

State benefits, the Ohio Veterans Bonus, and the Ohio Veterans Homes.

  • 877-OHIO-VET (877-644-6838)
  • dvs.ohio.gov

Homeless or at risk?

The National Call Center for Homeless Veterans answers 24/7.

  • 877-424-3838

Military OneSource

Free support for service members, Guard and Reserve, and their families.

  • 800-342-9647
  • militaryonesource.mil

The American Legion

Accredited Legion service officers help with VA claims and appeals at no cost, whether or not you're a member.

  • legion.org
