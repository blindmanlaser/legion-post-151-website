Funeral Honors

A Final Salute

The Post 151 Honor Guard is honored to render military honors at the funeral or memorial service of any veteran who served honorably. Honors may include a rifle salute, the playing of Taps, and the folding and presentation of the flag to the family.

There is no charge to the family. Families and funeral directors can send a request with the form below, and someone from the Post will contact you to confirm.

If you are working with a funeral home, they can also arrange honors through the veteran's branch of service.
