What Happens Next

  1. Send your payment. $200 per flag, cash or check made out to Sons of the American Legion Post 151. Drop it off at the Post, or mail it to: Conneaut American Legion, Attn: Hometown Hero Banners, 272 Broad St., Conneaut, OH 44030.
  2. Review your proof. We'll contact you to look over the flag design before it's printed.
  3. See your hero honored on Conneaut's streets from Memorial Day through Veterans Day.

Questions? Email hero-banners@conneautlegionpost151.org.
