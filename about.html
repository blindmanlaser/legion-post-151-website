About the Post

Our Post

Cowle Post 151 is home to Conneaut's American Legion family. We support veterans and their families, honor those who served, and give back to our community.

Our Legion Family

  • The American Legion Veterans who served honorably in the U.S. Armed Forces.
  • American Legion Auxiliary Family members of Veterans, supporting the Post and its programs.
  • Sons of the American Legion Male descendants of Legion-eligible Veterans.

Become a Member

Eligible Veterans and family members are welcome to join. See who can join and yearly dues, ask any officer, or stop by the Post.
